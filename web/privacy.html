Privacy policy

This is a self-hosted OpenDM instance. It replies to Instagram comments and direct messages on behalf of the account owner who installed it.

It stores, in the owner's own Cloudflare account, the Instagram usernames and IDs of people who comment a keyword or message the account, the text of those messages, and an email address only if the person chooses to send one. Nothing is shared with third parties. The owner can delete any of it from the Cloudflare D1 database.

To have your data removed, message the Instagram account or contact its owner.
